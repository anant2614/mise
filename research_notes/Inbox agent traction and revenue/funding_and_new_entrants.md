# Inbox agent funding, revenue disclosures and new entrants (Jan–Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-11. About 14 tool calls. Coverage is partial (see Gaps). Many 2026 figures come only from aggregators or SEO review blogs, and those are flagged.

## 1. 2026 rounds and revenue disclosures for named players and new entrants

### Takeaway
The main new item is that Instinct raised again: a $1B Series C at a $10B valuation (Sept 28, 2026), about a month after its $350M round. However, TechCrunch describes it as an SMS-based general personal agent and does not mention email, and it discloses no ARR or user numbers. In the inbox/EA category itself, 2026 money has gone almost entirely into seed-stage rounds of $3–7M: AgentMail ($6M, Mar), Upstream ($3M, Jun), Scape ($3.2M, Jul), Serif ($6.5M, ~Jul). Of these, only Serif (1,600 paid SMB subscribers) and AgentMail (500+ B2B customers) disclosed hard traction numbers. Fyxer is the only company with a 2026 ARR figure larger than its 2025 one ($35M), and that figure is unverified.

### Cited Findings
**Instinct (general personal agent, adjacent to the category)**
- Sept 28, 2026: $1B Series C at a $10B valuation from Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue. It came about a month after the round that valued it at $2.5B, and The Information first reported the talks. The invite-only service launched in Aug 2026. Users reach it by SMS/text and there is no mobile app. Its tasks are travel and restaurant booking, purchases, bill pay, canceling subscriptions, research and groceries. New features: a "concierge" that makes phone calls, and a "trusted person network" in which agents coordinate with each other. **Instinct has not disclosed user counts, ARR or pricing, and the article does not mention email.** Its privacy policy drew criticism and has since been updated. Competitor named: Meta's "Muse" assistant, which reached the top of the US app stores with millions of downloads — [TechCrunch, 2026-09-28](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/viral-ai-agent-instinct-raises-1b-series-c-at-a-10b-valuation/)
- Secondary coverage of the earlier round reports conflicting sizes ($250M Series B vs $350M) at a $2.5B valuation — [dutchstartup.ai](https://www.dutchstartup.ai/en/news/instinct-raises-250-million-for-personal-ai-assistant-now-valued-at-2-5-billion); [TechCrunch 2026-08-26](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/viral-ai-startup-instinct-has-raised-350-million-at-a-2-5-billion-valuation/)

**Fyxer**
- arr.club claims Fyxer "grew from 1M to 35M [ARR] in 1 year", in an entry dated May 20, 2026. **Unverified aggregator figure; I found no company statement or press confirmation** — [arr.club](https://www.arr.club/fyxer/fyxer-reaches-17m-arr)
- Entrepreneur UK's UK100 profile (Mar 2026) says Fyxer "hit $25m ARR in 10 months in 2025" — [Entrepreneur UK](https://uk.entrepreneur.com/entrepreneurs/uk100-start-ups-fyxer-ai). Welcome to the Jungle says $1M to $22M in 10 months — [WTTJ](https://app.welcometothejungle.com/companies/Fyxer). **Superhuman (formerly Grammarly)**
- June 23, 2026: Superhuman agreed to acquire GPTZero, an AI-detection company. Terms were not disclosed. GPTZero had 19M+ registered users and about $30M ARR, and its ~30 staff are joining. Its detection will be built into Superhuman Go, which works across about 1M apps and sites — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2200290/superhuman-has-acquired-ai-authenticity-service-gptzero); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/?p=407082); [Superhuman community post](https://connect.superhuman.com/t/exciting-news-superhuman-is-acquiring-gptzero/60894)
- Superhuman Go was free for existing Grammarly subscribers and moved to a paid subscription model on Feb 1, 2026 — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/de/news/grammarly-superhuman-ai-acquisition/)
- I found no 2026 revenue figure for Superhuman Mail or Go. The ~$35M ARR for Superhuman Mail and the $700M+ company revenue are 2025-era numbers repeated on SEO and stat sites — [sqmagazine (aggregator)](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/grammarly-ai-statistics/)
- BetaKit mentions a class-action suit filed earlier in 2026 over a feature that generated editing suggestions in the style of notable authors — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/?p=407082)

**AgentMail (infrastructure: email inboxes for agents, YC S25)**
- Mar 10, 2026: $6M seed led by General Catalyst, with YC and Phosphor. Angels include Paul Graham, Dharmesh Shah, Paul Copplestone and Karim Atiyeh. Traction: "tens of thousands" of human users, "hundreds of thousands" of agent users and 500+ B2B customers. Its user count tripled in the week OpenClaw/Clawdbot launched (late Jan 2026) and quadrupled in February. Revenue was not disclosed — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/agentmail-raises-6m-to-build-an-email-service-for-ai-agents/)

**Upstream (Paris, YC-backed)**
- June 3, 2026: $3M pre-seed from YC, Connect Ventures and 30+ founders and operators (from Framer, Algolia, Asana, Alan and Webflow). It pitches "the first inbox built for humans and AI agents simultaneously" with MCP connectivity. Team of 7 at Station F. "Thousands of users" tried the invite-only beta. No revenue or pricing disclosed — [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/yc-backed-french-startup-upstream-raises-3m-to-rebuild-email-in-the-era-of-ai-agents/)

**Scape (Stockholm, YC)**
- July 2026: $3.2M seed from General Catalyst, YC and angels. It is an AI-first email client that drafts replies before a thread is opened, reads meeting notes, and creates or edits attachments. No traction disclosed — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/scape-2/funding-rounds/seed-2026-07); [TAMradar](https://www.tamradar.com/funding-rounds/scape-seed-3-2m) (the Dealroom page returned 403)

**Serif (SF, founded by Kevin Yang and James Liu, Stanford grads)**
- ~July 2026: $6.5M from General Catalyst and angels. **1,600 paid small-business subscribers across the US, UK and Germany.** This comes from a search snippet of a UK accounting trade article that I did not fetch in full, so the exact date and round type are unconfirmed — [AccountingWEB](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/231126)

**Sol (Sol Foundry, ex-CRED founders)**

**Calendly Callie**

Callie is still in beta, English-only, and sold on new "Plus" plans (one review cites $18/seat). The Help Center described a private beta as of May 2026 — [Calendly press release](https://calendly.com/newsroom/press-release/calendly-new-ai-product-suite); [Calendly Help Center](https://calendly.com/help/ai-scheduling-assistant-callie-overview); [usecarly review (pricing; vendor-adjacent)](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/calendly-review/)

**Shortwave, Cora/Every, Lindy, Martin**
- Shortwave: no 2026 round found. Tracxn lists about $9M total, with the last round in 2020 (Lightspeed, USV) — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/shortwave/__VgTw3b2F6DvtNNn_m-RCzXZKFvl6I34XTB7ZA7teCF0)

### Inferences
- On disclosed traction, no inbox/EA pure-play has shown evidence in 2026 of beating Fyxer. Fyxer's unverified $35M ARR (May 2026) would still lead the category. Superhuman Mail's ~$35M is a stale 2025 figure.
- The fastest capital and valuation growth is in **general personal agents** (Instinct: $2.5B to $10B in about a month), not email-specific tools. If Instinct or Meta's Muse add email, they become the main threat, but no traction data is public.
- Agent-native email infrastructure (AgentMail, and Upstream's "humans + agents" inbox) is a 2026 sub-theme driven by OpenClaw-style agents. AgentMail's growth multiples (3x in a week, 4x in a month) are the strongest verified growth signals, but they come without revenue.
- General Catalyst backed three 2026 email deals: AgentMail, Scape and Serif.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 ARR for Fyxer, Superhuman Mail/Go, Shortwave, Lindy, Howie, Blockit or Cora.
- I did not search Howie, Blockit, Catch (no relevant results), a16z speedrun, or Product Hunt's email category, because of the tool-call budget.
- Serif's details rest on a snippet. The article should be fetched to confirm the date and round.
- YC directory pages may lag. W26/S26 email startups could be tagged under other industries (e.g. "AI Assistant", "Productivity").

## 3. Pricing changes in 2026

### Takeaway
Per-seat subscription is still the norm. The 2026 moves are removing free tiers, putting AI behind paid add-on tiers (Superhuman Go went paid on Feb 1, 2026; Calendly put Callie on Plus plans), and Lindy's hybrid of seats plus a pooled credit balance. I found no outcome-based pricing.

### Cited Findings
- Superhuman Go moved from free-with-Grammarly to a paid subscription on Feb 1, 2026 — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/de/news/grammarly-superhuman-ai-acquisition/)
- Calendly Callie is available only on new Plus plans (reported at $18/seat) — [Calendly](https://calendly.com/newsroom/press-release/calendly-new-ai-product-suite); [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/calendly-review/)
- One source says that by late July 2026, none of Shortwave, Fyxer or Cora had a permanent free plan (single secondary source). Another review still describes a Shortwave free tier with limited AI quota, so the two conflict. Shortwave Pro is about $18/mo and Team/Business is $30/seat/mo — [aiemaily](https://aiemaily.com/blog/free-ai-email-assistant-what-you-give-up); [get-alfred](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/shortwave-pricing); [cmdk.email](https://cmdk.email/post/shortwave-review/)
- Cora costs $20/mo — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/cora-ai/)
- Lindy charges $29.99–$199.99/mo per seat with a pooled credit balance, a seat-plus-usage hybrid (review site) — [ainora.lt](https://ainora.lt/blog/lindy-ai-review-alternatives-2026)

### Inferences
- The trend is to monetize AI by removing free AI quotas and adding AI tiers to existing seats, rather than to adopt usage or outcome pricing.

### Gaps
- Most pricing evidence comes from competitor-written review blogs (usecarly, get-alfred, aiemaily), which have an interest in how competitors are portrayed, so the figures should be verified against the official pages.
- I found no 2026 Fyxer, Howie or Blockit pricing changes.
